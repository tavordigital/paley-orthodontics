For parents

The first orthodontic check-up, by age 7

The American Association of Orthodontists recommends that children have their first orthodontic check-up by age 7. While orthodontics is often associated with pre-teens and teenagers, there are compelling reasons to evaluate your child's orthodontic needs much sooner.

  1. 1
    Early detection of subtle issues. Orthodontists can identify subtle problems with jaw growth and emerging teeth even while baby teeth are still present.
  2. 2
    Unseen issues. Your child's teeth may appear straight, but underlying problems can be detected only by an orthodontist.
  3. 3
    Accurate assessment. The check-up may reveal that your child's bite is fine. If a developing problem is identified, Dr. Paley may recommend monitoring your child's growth and development, starting treatment at the optimal time if needed.
  4. 4
    Prevent serious problems. Early treatment can prevent more serious issues from developing, potentially making later treatment shorter and less complicated.
  5. 5
    Optimal results. In some cases, early intervention allows for results that may not be achievable once the face and jaws have finished growing.

Benefits of early treatment

  • Guide jaw growth
  • Lower the risk of trauma to protruding front teeth
  • Correct harmful oral habits
  • Improve appearance and the way the lips meet
  • Guide permanent teeth into more favorable positions
  • Correct crossbites

Each child is unique in their physiological development and treatment needs. Dr. Paley's goal is to provide the most appropriate treatment at the most appropriate time for every patient.

For adults

Two out of every five orthodontic patients are over 21.

Orthodontic treatment is effective at any age, and adults especially value the benefits of a beautiful smile and healthy teeth. At Dr. Paley's office, we offer clear ceramic braces and Invisalign® as discreet alternatives to traditional metal braces.

Improved oral hygiene
Straightening crooked teeth makes it easier to clean your teeth and floss effectively, promoting better oral health.
Facilitate restorations
Proper alignment can place teeth in better positions, aiding in the restoration of missing or lost teeth.

Dr. Paley collaborates closely with your general dentist and other specialists to ensure optimal results, providing you with a beautiful and healthy smile.
